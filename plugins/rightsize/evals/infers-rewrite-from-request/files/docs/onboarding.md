# New Employee Onboarding Process v3.2

## Purpose

This document defines the comprehensive, end-to-end onboarding journey for every new team member at Fernleaf, ensuring a world-class experience from day one. Fernleaf has 6 employees and hires about two people a year.

## Governance

The Onboarding Steering Committee (CEO, CTO and office manager) meets every Monday to review the status of every onboarding in progress. Each onboarding is tracked as a Jira epic with one ticket per step below, and each ticket needs a sign-off from the committee before the next step starts.

## Steps

1. HR sends the contract through DocuSign.
2. The office manager orders a laptop.
3. The committee approves the laptop specification.
4. IT installs the standard image and turns on full-disk encryption.
5. The committee approves the account list.
6. IT creates the Google Workspace, Slack and GitHub accounts.
7. The new hire completes the security training and signs the acceptable use policy.
8. IT grants production access only after step 7 is signed, because our SOC 2 audit checks this.
9. The buddy schedules a welcome lunch.
10. The CTO records a personal welcome video.
11. The new hire fills in a 40-question satisfaction survey after week 1, week 4 and week 12.
12. The committee reviews the survey results and updates this document.

## Future work
