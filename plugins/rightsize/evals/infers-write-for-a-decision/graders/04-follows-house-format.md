The repository's existing records use a numbered title ("# 1. ...") and the headings Date, Status, Context, Decision and Consequences.
PASS if the new record uses a numbered title for record 3 and the same headings or fields, possibly with one or two extra sections such as options or a revisit trigger.
FAIL if it uses a different structure, such as a full design doc with rollout, testing or architecture sections.
