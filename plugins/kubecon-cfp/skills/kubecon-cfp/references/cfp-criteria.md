# KubeCon CFP Scoring Criteria & Rules

## Character Limits

| Field | Limit |
|-------|-------|
| Title | 75 characters |
| Abstract | 1,300 characters |
| Benefits to Ecosystem | 1,000-1,500 characters |

## Session Formats

| Format | Duration | Speakers | Notes |
|--------|----------|----------|-------|
| Session Presentation | 30 min | 1-2 | Most common |
| Panel Discussion | 30 min | 3-5 | Must have gender diversity, all named at submission |
| Lightning Talk | 5 min | 1 | Lower competition, great entry point |
| Tutorial | 75 min | 1-5 | Hands-on, needs clear prerequisites |

## Scoring Dimensions (1-5 stars, half-star increments)

### Content (weight: high)
- Is the proposal relevant and coherent?
- Quality of the proposal itself
- Likelihood of effective delivery
- Clear structure and logical flow

### Originality (weight: high)
- New or innovative ideas/approaches
- Originality of delivery method
- Not previously presented at CNCF events

### Relevance (weight: medium)
- New and exciting insights for the community
- Relevant to the specific conference and track
- Timely topic

### Speaker(s) (weight: medium)
- Demonstrated expertise in subject matter
- Alignment between speaker background and topic
- Supplemental materials (previous talks, blog posts)

## Selection Process

1. **Program Committee** — 5+ reviewers per proposal, mandatory comments
2. **Track Chairs** — rank top ~30% using comparison mode
3. **Co-Chairs** — final balance: gender diversity, company representation, first-time speakers (30-40% target)

## Hard Rules

- Max 3 submissions per person (exceeding = ALL removed)
- No previously presented content from past year at CNCF/LF events
- No all-male panels (3+ speakers)
- No sales pitches or product marketing
- Third person in abstract
- No AI-generated submissions (risk of ban)

## Pre-Submit Checklist

- [ ] Title: specific, intriguing, ≤75 characters, title case
- [ ] Abstract: third person, complete sentences, hook→promise→payoff, ≤1,300 chars
- [ ] Track: correct track selected, content matches track description
- [ ] Benefits: distinct from abstract, explains ecosystem value, 1,000-1,500 chars
- [ ] Supplemental: video recordings, blog posts, GitHub repos linked
- [ ] No sales/marketing language
- [ ] Content is original (not presented at CNCF in past year)
- [ ] All open-source projects listed with links
- [ ] Reviewed by experienced speaker
- [ ] Max 3 total submissions
- [ ] Panel diversity (if applicable)
- [ ] Concrete takeaways stated
- [ ] Specific metrics/numbers included
