# Reporting security incidents

It is important to note that, notwithstanding any other provision of this runbook, the on-call engineer shall notify the security team in writing without undue delay and, in any event, not later than forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any actual or suspected incident that affects, or is reasonably likely to affect, customer data, unless the incident is limited to staging environments. Additionally, this ensures a robust and seamless response process.
