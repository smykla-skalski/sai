## What Hiring Managers Scan For

Priority order:

1. **Organizational influence evidence** — changed how org works, not just built features
2. **Technical judgment** — chose right problems, not just solved them
3. **Architecture ownership** — owned direction, defended trade-offs
4. **Quantified business impact** — $, %, scale, time, reliability
5. **Leadership scope** — team size, cross-functional, mentoring seniors
6. **Role-relevant tech stack** — match, not just list
7. **Clear career progression** — growing scope with each role

## Senior vs Staff Language

| Senior Signal | Staff Signal |
|--------------|-------------|
| "Built X feature" | "Architected X system adopted by N teams" |
| "Improved performance by X%" | "Defined performance strategy reducing p99 by X% across Y services" |
| "Led team of N" | "Built and grew team from 0 to N, establishing hiring bar for domain" |
| "Used Kafka for messaging" | "Designed event-driven architecture processing Xm events/day" |
| "Wrote documentation" | "Established documentation-as-product strategy, reducing support tickets X%" |
| "On-call for production" | "Served as incident commander, established post-incident review process" |
| "Contributed to open source" | "Core maintainer of CNCF project (Xk stars, X00+ contributors)" |

## Optimal Resume Structure

```
Name / Contact / Links (GitHub, LinkedIn, talks)

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY (3-4 lines, Staff positioning)

TECHNICAL EXPERTISE (categorized by domain)

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE (reverse chronological, Staff-framed)

OPEN SOURCE & TECHNICAL LEADERSHIP (separate section!)

SPEAKING & COMMUNITY (separate, prominent)

EDUCATION (brief, last)
```

## XYZ Bullet Formula

```
Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]
```

Staff-level additions to XYZ:
- Add scope: "across N teams" / "company-wide" / "for N services"
- Add decision: "evaluated X vs Y, chose Z because..."
- Add lasting impact: "established as standard" / "adopted org-wide"

## Common Mistakes

1. **Title mismatch** — Senior title but claiming Staff scope (address with scope language, not title inflation)
2. **Buried differentiators** — OSS contributions, conference talks hidden in job bullets
3. **AI skill inflation** — "basic LLM knowledge" signals user, not builder
4. **Assumed skills listed** — Git, SQL, basic tools dilute at Staff level
5. **Gerund weakness** — "Driving initiative" (ongoing?) vs "Drove initiative" (completed)
6. **Fragment bullets** — "200M+ profiles" needs context, metrics, and impact
7. **Duplicate achievements** — Two bullets describing same work from different angles

## ATS Optimization

- Single column, standard fonts
- Standard section headers (Professional Summary, not "About Me")
- Both acronyms and spelled-out (Kubernetes (K8s), CI/CD)
- PDF for human review; some ATS prefer .docx
- No tables, graphics, images, multi-column layouts
- Mirror exact phrases from job description naturally

## Staff Archetypes

Determine which archetype the target role maps to:

- **Tech Lead:** Day-to-day technical guidance, team execution
- **Architect:** Owns technical direction of critical area
- **Solver:** Tackles arbitrarily complex problems
- **Right Hand:** Extends executive attention across complex orgs

Archetype alignment drives which bullets to lead with:
- Tech Lead role → lead with team/execution bullets
- Architect role → lead with design/strategy bullets
- Solver role → lead with complex problem-solving bullets
- Right Hand role → lead with cross-org coordination bullets
